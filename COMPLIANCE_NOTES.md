# Compliance notes

Single place to review every legal, clinical, and content placeholder in this
codebase before launch. Nothing listed under **Open placeholder markers** may
ship. The table is generated from the source, so line numbers are exact.

```bash
node scripts/compliance-scan.mjs                 # print the current list
node scripts/compliance-scan.mjs --update-notes  # refresh the table below
node scripts/compliance-scan.mjs --strict        # launch gate: exits 1 while any marker remains
```

The scan always exits non-zero if a forbidden phrase appears anywhere.

## Standing rules (apply to every page, current and future)

1. **Forbidden phrases: none.** The original brief barred "OB/GYN
   prescribed" and "bioidentical"; the client lifted both on 2026-09-05 and
   confirmed the wording may be used freely. The scanner's `FORBIDDEN` list
   in `scripts/compliance-scan.mjs` is empty but kept so a phrase can be
   barred again in one line. Where either term is used it describes the
   clinicians or the formulation, so counsel should confirm accuracy in the
   same review pass as the claim markers.
2. **Soha is not the dispensing pharmacy.** The dispensing pharmacy's legal
   name and address appear on the medication label; Soha branding is
   secondary packaging only. No copy may state or imply otherwise. Wherever
   the pharmacy is named, `[PHARMACY_NAME_PLACEHOLDER]` stands in for it.
3. **"One instead of three" is a convenience claim only.** Copy may say a
   single regimen is simpler to manage than separate prescriptions. It may not
   say or imply the combination is safer, more effective, or medically
   superior.
4. **No invented content.** No testimonials, statistics, doctor names,
   outcomes, or prices anywhere except clearly labelled placeholders.
5. **Dosing, mg amounts, and comparative claims** are marked
   `CLAIM_PENDING_LEGAL_REVIEW` at the exact point they would appear and are
   left blank until counsel and the clinical lead approve wording.
6. **Developer-authored copy** (anything not supplied verbatim in the brief)
   is marked `COPY_DRAFT` at the definition site so brand and legal can review
   it in one pass. Short UI labels (nav items, button text, footer column
   headings) are not individually marked; they are listed below.

## Pre-launch switches (not placeholders, but must be flipped)

- `src/app/layout.tsx` — `robots: { index: false, follow: false }` keeps the
  site out of search engines while it carries placeholders. Set to `true` at
  launch. `/start`, `/privacy` and `/terms` also carry their own page-level
  `noindex`; decide at launch which of those should stay out of the index
  (legal pages are usually indexed, the intake entry usually is not).
- `src/lib/intake/validate.ts` — the 18+ eligibility rule is a developer
  assumption (`MIN_AGE`). Confirm with counsel and the medical director.
- `src/config/states.ts` — `servedStates` is `null` (not gated). Set the list
  of served state codes before launch; pharmacy partners have restrictions.
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_ANALYTICS=on` enables Vercel Analytics; leave unset until
  launch.
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` — set in Vercel so canonical/OG URLs are absolute.
- `/privacy` and `/terms` are linked from the footer and will be stub pages
  until counsel supplies final documents.
- The testimonial block on the homepage
  (`src/components/home/SocialProofPlaceholder.tsx`) is intentionally ugly and
  carries `data-not-for-launch="true"`. Delete or replace it; do not restyle it.
